9 Men's Morris

Play against the computer, locally, or online with a friend.

How to play

Players take turns placing pieces on empty points. After you have placed your first three pieces, each turn may either place another piece or move one of your pieces to an adjacent connected point.

  1. Form a mill by lining up three of your pieces on a marked board line.
  2. After forming a mill, remove one opponent piece that is not in a mill when possible.
  3. Continue placing until all nine pieces are on the board; movement remains available.
  4. If you have only three pieces after placing all nine, you may fly to any empty point.
  5. After an opponent has placed all nine pieces, you win if they have fewer than three pieces or no legal move.

A piece cannot move back and forth along the same edge more than two consecutive cycles.
